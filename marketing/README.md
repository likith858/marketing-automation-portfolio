6. **Reel scripts:** 15-30 second scripts with visual, audio and on-screen text
7. **Creative test plan:** 5 one-variable A/B tests with decisions
8. **Landing page:** full copy, each line traced to customer research
9. **Proof & CTA:** button microcopy and objection-busting proof lines
10. **Follow-up sequence:** WhatsApp + email lead nurture with stop rules
