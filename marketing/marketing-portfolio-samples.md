## 2. Ad Review: 8-point checklist

*Each point scored 0 (missing), 1 (weak), 2 (strong). Total /16. 13-16 keep, 9-12 improve, 0-8 rewrite.*

| # | Check | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | Does the first line stop the scroll? |
| 2 | One audience | Is it clearly for ONE person? |
| 3 | One pain | Does it name one real problem in their words? |
| 4 | Specific benefit | Is the result concrete, not vague? |
| 5 | Proof | Is there one true trust point? |
| 6 | Clear CTA | One easy, low-risk action? |
| 7 | Short + clear | Understood in 5 seconds on a phone? |
| 8 | Honest | No fake claims or invented results? |

| Ad | Business | First version | Score | Weakest point |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Gym | We care after you pay too / Once you pay, do they stop caring? | 10 | Two benefits, long, no proof |
| 3 | Gym | Trainers who correct your form / Tired of trainers who just roam around? | 10 | Vague results promise |
| 4 | Gym | Stay consistent this time / Before: you join, lose motivation in 2 weeks... | 11 | Weak 'Before:' hook, 3 benefits |
| 5 | Coaching | Know where you lose marks / Writing tests every week but your score is still stuck? | 13 | CTA weaker than the offer |
| 6 | AC repair | AC fixed today, fixed price / Before: AC not cooling... | 13 | Hook starts with a label |
| 7 | Coaching | Poster: Try the class before you pay. | 11 | No audience, no pain |
| 13 | Gym | Reel: Buying a gym membership is easy. Doing the right exercise... | 11 | Hook too long, two ideas |

## 10. Lead follow-up sequence: coaching institute

*Stop rules: end the sequence when the lead replies, books, joins or opts out. Hot leads also get a call within 1 hour.*

| Step | Channel | When | Message | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Instant reply | WhatsApp | Within 5 min | Hi [first name], thanks for your enquiry about the [course] batch. I'm [counsellor]. Are you preparing for your 1st attempt, or have you written Prelims before? | Reply 1 or 2 |
| 1b. Welcome | Email | Within 10 min | Subject A: [First name], your [course] enquiry: what happens next. Body: counsellor call → free demo class → study-plan suggestion. | Book a demo |
| Hot leads | Call | Within 1 hour | Confirm course and attempt → ask the hardest part of their preparation → match one feature → offer a demo slot → log in the sheet. | Book on the call |
| 2. Value | WhatsApp | Day 1, 6-8 PM | Sharing our free 1-page Prelims revision plan: [link]. Which subject do you find hardest right now? | Download + reply |
| 3. Objection | Email | Day 3 | Is coaching worth it if you're self-studying? Self-study builds knowledge; it can't check your answers. [Weekly evaluation] + [mentor session]. Try a free demo first. | Book a demo |
| 4. Last call | WhatsApp | Day 7 | Would you like a demo slot for the [date] batch, or should I check back closer to your exam? Either answer is fine. | Reply DEMO or LATER |
