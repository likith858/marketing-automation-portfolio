# Funnel Maps (Sample Work)

8-stage customer funnels for 3 local businesses, plus funnel math to find the biggest leak.

> Business names are samples. Anything in [brackets] is a placeholder to confirm with a real client. Funnel numbers are **simulated** practice data.

## 1. Funnel stages

| Stage | Sample IAS Academy (coaching) | Sample Fitness Studio (gym) | Sample AC Service (repair) | Why this stage matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Traffic | Reel "Stuck below 70 in mocks?" + poster "Notes are free. Feedback isn't." Target: UPSC aspirants 21-30 in the city | Reel "Crowded gym? No one checks your form." + poster "New to the gym? Start here." Target: 20-35, within 5 km | Google Search ad "AC repair near me" + Meta ad "AC not cooling? Same-day technician" (April-June) | No traffic = no leads. The ad must reach the right person |
| 2. Landing page | Hook → 3 benefits (mentor checks answers, small batch, evening batch) → [real proof] → free 1:1 answer review → form | Hook → limited slots + live crowd timings → trainer guidance → [member video] → free trial → form | Fixed price before work → verified technicians → 30-day warranty → [Google rating] → form + WhatsApp button | Must match the ad's promise, or people leave in seconds |
| 3. Lead magnet / offer | Free 10-min 1:1 Mains answer review (or free beginner booklist PDF) | [Free trial session] with a trainer + beginner 4-week plan PDF | Free price quote on WhatsApp in 5 minutes | Gives a reason to share a phone number |
| 4. Form | Name, phone, email, course (4 fields) | Name, phone, goal | Name, phone, area, AC type | Every extra field makes more people give up |
| 5. Thank-you page | "A mentor will WhatsApp you within 1 hour to fix your review slot." | "Your trainer will WhatsApp you within 2 hours to fix your trial time." | "Your price quote is coming on WhatsApp in 5 minutes." | Confirms it worked and says what happens next |
| 6. Instant follow-up | n8n: save lead + welcome email + WhatsApp within 5 min | n8n: save lead + WhatsApp "What's your main goal?" + welcome email | n8n: save lead + WhatsApp fixed-price quote within 5 min | Fast replies win the lead |
| 7. Booking + show-up | Slot fixed on WhatsApp → reminder 2 hrs before | Trial booked online → reminder with map + trainer name | Technician name + photo sent before the visit | A booked lead who doesn't show up is still lost |
| 8. Sale + retention | Batch offer → review request when scores improve → [referral offer] | Membership offer → day-30 review request → weekly progress check | Google review request same evening → next-summer service reminder | Happy customers stay and bring new ones |

## 2. Funnel math (simulated)

| Step | IAS Academy | Fitness Studio | AC Service |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clicked the ad | 1,000 | 800 | 600 |
| Filled the form | 100 (10%) | 120 (15%) | 90 (15%) |
| Booked | 40 (40%) | 60 (50%) | 54 (60%) |
| Showed up | 25 (62%) | 30 (50%) | 50 (93%) |
| Paid | 8 (32%) | 12 (40%) | 35 (70%) |

## 3. Biggest leak → fix

| Business | Biggest leak | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| IAS Academy | Leads → booked: 60 of 100 never booked | WhatsApp reply within 5 min; call hot leads within 1 hour (n8n) |
| Fitness Studio | Booked → showed up: half didn't come | Reminder 2 hrs before with trainer name + map; ask them to reply OK |
| AC Service | Clicks → leads: only 15% filled the form | Show the price range on the page + 1-tap WhatsApp button |

**Rule:** fix the step with the biggest drop first. It's cheaper than buying more ads.
