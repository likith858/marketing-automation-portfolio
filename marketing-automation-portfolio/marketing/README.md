# Marketing samples

`copy-bank-samples.pdf` contains:

- **Hook Book:** 30 ad hooks across 8 formulas, rated, top 5 marked
- **Ad Copy:** Meta ads written with PAS, AIDA and BAB
- **Landing Page:** full landing-page copy (8 sections) with a source column tracing each line to customer research
- **Proof & CTA:** CTA buttons with microcopy, review-request messages, objection-busting proof lines
- **Follow-ups:** 4-step email and WhatsApp sequences with A/B subject lines, metrics and the n8n node that sends each step
- **Creative Concepts:** ad concepts with format, ratio, 3-second hook and on-screen text, plus reviewed corrections

Samples prepared for practice and pitches (EKAM IAS Academy, LFG Gym, Himayatnagar) are not the businesses' current marketing. Anything in [brackets] is a placeholder to confirm with the business.
